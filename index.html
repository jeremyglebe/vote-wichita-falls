Bring photo ID and a paper list of your choices. You may read your notes in the booth. Keep your phone put away. ID and paper-guide rules

What to bring

Passport cards count. Bring your physical ID.

AgeExpired ID
18–69Accepted photo ID may be up to 4 years expired.
70+Any expiration age is allowed if the ID is otherwise valid.
Accepted IDs and alternatives

Accepted: Texas DPS driver license, personal ID card, election ID certificate or handgun license; U.S. passport book or card; U.S. military photo ID; or U.S. citizenship certificate with photo. Citizenship certificates do not expire.

If you do not have and cannot reasonably get an accepted photo ID, bring an approved supporting document and complete a Reasonable Impediment Declaration. Supporting documents include a current utility bill, bank statement or voter registration certificate.

Official ID rules

Paper guides are allowed in the booth

You may bring handwritten notes, a printed guide or a marked sample ballot for your own use.

Do not hand out guides, display them as signs or campaign to other voters inside the marked 100-foot polling-place boundary or 20-foot curbside-voting boundary.

Keep phones put away in the voting room. No photos or recordings within 100 feet of a voting station.

What the tags mean

Tags flag political positions, money and connections. Open “Details & sources” under a candidate for the evidence.

  • Nazis / far right: white-nationalist ties, Nazi-style propaganda or Christian-nationalist politics. The tag names the connection.
  • Tech bros: billionaire backing. Data-center support is labeled separately.
  • Zionism: support for Israel, arms or anti-boycott laws. Never based on religion.
  • Endorsements: donations, jobs and appointments are labeled separately.
